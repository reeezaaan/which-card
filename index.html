How to rank them

1.0¢ if you take statement credit. Around 2¢ is typical through Points Boost or a transfer to Hyatt.
Delta prices dynamically. 1.2¢ is a fair long-run average; flash sales beat it, last-minute fares don't.
Percentages are the earn rate, not a guarantee of value. A Chase point is worth 1¢ cashed out and more if you transfer it, so treat the percentage as the floor. Check the rates before you rely on them. Issuers change categories constantly — the Sapphire Reserve was rebuilt in 2025 and no longer pays a bonus on general travel, which is the single biggest change reflected here. Every multiplier on the My cards tab is editable and saves in this browser, so correct anything that has moved. Merchant categories are the usual coding, not a guarantee: the merchant's own MCC decides, and it can differ by location and by how you check out.
